# Third-party software and media

Speech synthesis uses [wangwangit/tts](https://github.com/wangwangit/tts), pinned to `749645c46df7e080561ad0585240f2a29c53e554`. It is MIT-licensed, copyright (c) 2025 一只会飞的旺旺. Its Microsoft speech REST bridge is executed locally; its source is fetched into an ignored vendor folder when synthesis is requested. No operator credentials are distributed.

FFmpeg, Pillow, NumPy and OpenCV retain their own licenses and are installed separately. The Opus 5.5 reference [PDoomVideo](https://github.com/JohnHeibel/PDoomVideo) is linked for context; its code, song and video are not copied into this repository.

The truck cue uses a newly synthesized short phrase from Beethoven's public-domain Für Elise, rather than a downloaded recording. The sparse piano accompaniment and sound effects are original procedural audio. The story narration is Microsoft synthesized speech; it is not a recording of any real actor.
